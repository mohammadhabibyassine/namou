## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-performance technical apparel and modular urban gear through an intersection of cybernetic minimalism, archival streetwear, and utilitarian brutalism. The interface functions as an operating system for tactical gear deployment, emphasizing calculated precision, rugged materiality, and industrial ergonomics.

The aesthetic marries concrete-cast physical presence with high-readability tactical telemetry. Surfaces feel architectural and dense, anchored by warm stone and sharp carbon panels, then energized by high-visibility volt accents that function like hazard and safety indicators. The emotional response is grounded, razor-sharp, and uncompromised: every pixel, reticle, and line of metadata acts as structural telemetry rather than arbitrary ornamentation.

## Colors

The palette establishes an industrial balance between raw architectural tones and high-visibility field markers:

- **Primary (`#D4FF00`)**: Electric Lime / Volt Chartreuse. Reserved for focal interaction points, active states, crosshair markers, critical toggles, and high-priority system alerts. Never used as large background fills; it serves strictly as an energetic catalyst against neutral planes.
- **Secondary (`#111111`)**: Deep Carbon Obsidian. Used for commanding display typography, primary call-to-action buttons, high-contrast inverted cards, and anchor modules.
- **Tertiary (`#666660`)**: Slate Graphite. Applied to secondary labels, technical specs, coordinates, dimension lines, and passive telemetry.
- **Neutral (`#E7E4DC`)**: Warm Concrete / Bone Stone. The foundational canvas surface. It offers organic warmth compared to synthetic clinical whites, establishing an archival, tactical atmosphere.

### Extended Tone Matrix

- **Canvas Base**: `#E7E4DC` (Warm Concrete)
- **Canvas Alternate**: `#EDEAE2` (Bone Stone)
- **Surface Elevation Light**: `#DBD8D0` (Dense Mineral) and `#ECE9E1` (Soft Stone)
- **Surface Elevation Dark**: `#111111` (Carbon Obsidian) and `#0A0A0A` (Deep Void)
- **Technical Hairlines & Borders**: `#D2CEBF` (Calibrated Ochre-Stone) on light surfaces; `#262624` on obsidian surfaces.
- **Surface Text Inverted**: `#FFFFFF` exclusively for dark containers and obsidian modules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces tactical rigor through three dedicated type families:

1. **Display & Collection Headlines (`Bebas Neue`)**: Massive, architectural, and tightly tracked. Headlines are always rendered in uppercase to emulate shipping container typography, industrial stamping, and technical specification binders.
2. **Body & Utility Copy (`Inter`)**: Clean, neutral, high-legibility geometric sans-serif that balances the raw impact of display headers. Optimized for product descriptions, sizing breakdowns, and transactional operations.
3. **Telemetry & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict, monospaced notation for coordinates, material weights, weather resistance tiers, SKU codes, and micro-labels. Rendered exclusively in uppercase with extended letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to evoke instrumentation readouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column tactical grid on desktop, transitioning to an 8-column grid on tablet, and a 4-column fluid module system on mobile.

- **Grid Lines & Registration Marks**: Structural containers align to hairline grid borders (`1px solid #D2CEBF`). Section intersections feature corner `+` registration reticles (8px x 8px) or L-brackets positioned at the bounding vertices.
- **Gutters & Margin Rhythm**: Tight, controlled rhythm. Mobile uses a strict 16px (`1rem`) outer margin with 12px card gaps to maximize edge-to-edge product scale. Desktop leverages 32px (`2rem`) gutters, allowing expansive modular arrangements that emulate architectural blueprints.
- **Reflow Protocols**: Content stacks vertically on mobile without reducing visual density. High-density data strips, technical attributes, and quick-add actions remain accessible via persistent bottom docked frames.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed entirely through tonal layering, tectonic stacking, and structural hairlines—diffuse drop shadows are strictly prohibited.

- **Tonal Stacking**: Depth is achieved by placing elevated components on distinct stone or carbon tiers. Base canvas (`#E7E4DC`) holds primary cards (`#DBD8D0`), while inverted focus modules punch through in full Carbon Obsidian (`#111111`).
- **Low-Contrast Precision Outlines**: All interactive cards, modules, and popovers employ a 1px solid hairline (`#D2CEBF` on light; `#262624` on dark) to establish distinct, machine-tooled perimeters.
- **Hard-Surface Overlays**: When modals or overlays trigger, use an obsidian scrim at 70% opacity (`rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.70)`) combined with a backdrop filter blur of `4px` to preserve the underlying architectural silhouette.
- **Active Reticle Glaze**: Active card states highlight with a 1px inner outline rendered in Electric Lime (`#D4FF00`).

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp industrial framing with high-ergonomic corner radii. Primary modules, cards, and modal containers use generous 16px to 24px corner smoothing (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`), creating the silhouette of molded cybernetic hardware and ballistic carry equipment.

Interactive chips, badges, and primary action triggers utilize full pill contours (`rounded-full`), contrasting against the rectilinear layout grid. Micro-elements—including thumbnail viewports, technical callouts, and data inputs—maintain a sharper, tighter corner radius (`8px`, `rounded-md`) to communicate structural solidity.

## Components

### Buttons & Interaction Triggers

- **Primary CTA**: Deep carbon (`#111111`) fill with white (`#FFFFFF`) or Electric Lime (`#D4FF00`) text in `JetBrains Mono` or uppercase `Inter`. Full pill or 16px radius. Includes a subtle right-aligned icon or `[+]` bracket marker. Hover triggers an instant color inversion to Electric Lime fill with Carbon Obsidian text.
- **Secondary Action**: Transparent background with a 1px `#111111` or `#D2CEBF` hairline border. Inverts subtly on touch/hover.
- **Quick-Add (`+`) Trigger**: Circular or square pill button (40px x 40px) pinned to bottom-right of mobile product tiles. Displays a sharp vector cross (`+`). On press, morphs with an Electric Lime flash.

### Filter Chips & Technical Badges

- **Pill Tags**: 24px height, full pill radius. Light stone surface (`#DBD8D0`) with Slate Graphite (`#666660`) metadata font (`tech-data-sm`).
- **Active Filter State**: Solid Carbon Obsidian (`#111111`) fill with Electric Lime (`#D4FF00`) text and an optional glowing status dot (4px x 4px).

### Product Cards & Modularity

- **Mobile Product Card**: Edge-to-edge full-bleed card with 20px radius. Dual corner brackets `[ ]` overlaid on imagery at 50% opacity. Features a top metadata rail showing gear volume/weight (e.g., `L-01 // 340 GSM`) and bottom floating bar with condensed price and quick-add trigger.
- **Inverted Tactical Card**: Solid `#111111` container housing white body text, green reticles, and monospaced geo-coordinates indicating testing grounds (e.g., `35°41'22.2"N 139°41'30.2"E`).

### Form Fields & Technical Inputs

- **Input Fields**: Recessed bone-stone surface (`#ECE9E1`) with a bottom 1px structural hairline `#D2CEBF`. Label floats above in `tech-data-sm` with a numeric index prefix (e.g., `01 // ENTER COMM ADDR`). Active state sharpens the bottom hairline to `#111111` or `#D4FF00`.

### Technical Framing & Telemetry Reticles

- **Corner Brackets**: 8px vector tick marks placed at `top-left`, `top-right`, `bottom-left`, `bottom-right` of hero blocks.
- **Telemetry Dials & Circular Reticles**: Decorative circular SVG rings depicting weather resistance ratings, breathability scores, or modular compatibility indices. Rendered in 1px stroke `#888880`.

### Navigation & Docked Bars

- **Mobile Header**: Minimalist 56px height, stone-tinted backdrop with 8px blur. Centered condensed uppercase logo with Left: Tactical Menu Trigger `[MENU]`, Right: Cart counter styled as `BAG [02]`.
- **Bottom Command Bar**: Utilitarian tab bar pinned to the bottom screen safe area. High-density layout using monospaced labels and geometric glyphs with Electric Lime active dot indicators.
